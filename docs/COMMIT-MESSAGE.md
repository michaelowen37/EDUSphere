Games: play first, a space shooter, swipe, drag and streaks

- Pass IM: the most playful game on a grade's list now opens first (funFirst), so every grade starts on Catch, Frog Jumps, Pong, balance, path or dots; grade 11 and college gained Step on the powers of 2 and Catch the primary sources. A new layout audit opens all games on five screens (small phone to laptop, and a phone sideways): nothing runs off the screen or spills out of a tile, and play boards fit a sideways phone. Decks corrected and matched to their lessons: grade 3 fraction twins, a Texas history deck for grade 7 (capitals moved to world geography), statistics words for college, five science definitions, and a clear solution and plain mixture for grade 5.
- Pass IN: the space shooter, Mikey's idea: tap to aim and the ship fires at the chips that fit the rule (keyboard too), in grade 2 numbers (even numbers), grade 3 science (gases) and grade 6 science (elements), where it now opens first. Grade 7 science, grade 12 science and grade 12 economics trade games that did not match their course for producers and consumers, igneous rocks, and needs and wants. Every new chip is named in its lesson's key idea first.
- Pass IO: the last four games that did not match their course now do (compound words in grade 2 reading, goods and services in grade 1 civics, positive sines in precalculus, mitosis or meiosis in biology); the robot walk sets its grid beside its controls on a sideways phone; a fun review of every kind in DECISIONS, with a swipe game planned for the quiz-like ones.
- Pass IP: Myth or fact, Green flag or red flag, Reason or not, and Valid or not play as swipe cards: drag the card left or right (it leans and shows its side), tap a button laid out the same way, or press an arrow key; a streak counts right answers in a row. Content, scoring and button names are unchanged.
- Pass IQ: Sort the ledger, Fit the room, Who gains and Which sense play by drag and drop: carry the card onto its box (each box lights as the card passes; a card let go elsewhere slides home), tap the box, or press its number; a streak counts right answers in a row. Content, scoring and button names are unchanged.
- Pass IR: every answer-picking game keeps a streak of right answers in a row; Find the tool and Name the feeling play by drag and drop (carry the question to its tool, the face to its feeling), with tapping and number keys still working.
