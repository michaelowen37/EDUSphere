# The Texas Education Freedom Accounts plan

Written September 25, 2026. The goal Mikey set: pre-K and kindergarten free, everything from grade 1 up sold, and The Wise Human approved as a vendor in the Texas Education Freedom Accounts marketplace (run by Odyssey for the Texas Comptroller) before branching out. Program rules change; check the official pages before each step.

## Where The Wise Human stands

**Stronger than most of the field**
- Pre-K 3 through college in one product, and the same classroom tools at every level.
- Built on the TEKS, with every history and science standard cited in docs/TEKS-CHECKLIST.md and a standards map inside the app. Most homeschool curricula are not written to the TEKS, and Texas families who may return to public school, or who want STAAR-shaped practice, care about that.
- Mastery with memory checks that return days later, not completion checkmarks.
- A story for every module and a long story for every course, games that practice each course's idea, and printable weekly notes, reports, transcripts and certificates for a homeschool record.
- No ads, no accounts on a server, nothing collected, and it works offline.

**Weaker, and worth saying plainly**
- It is a mastery curriculum, not yet a full year of daily lessons: about 23 to 43 modules per grade across all subjects, where a full-year core program carries well over a hundred lessons per subject. Sell it as a core-plus program for math, reading, science, writing and history practice, and grow toward full years.
- Not accredited. Several approved vendors advertise accreditation. Texas does not require it of homeschool curricula, but families like it; consider it after launch.
- The pictures are not painted yet, and pre-K and kindergarten, the free storefront, should be finished first.
- One device per classroom; siblings share it well, separate devices need sync later.
- No reviews or track record yet.

## Vendor readiness (iECHO, LLC)

1. Confirm iECHO is registered with the Texas Secretary of State and in good standing on Texas franchise tax.
2. Publish on iECHO's website: a product page, a privacy policy (nothing collected), terms of service, a refund policy and support contact.
3. Prepare the offering: title, grade range, subjects, delivery (a license code emailed after the order), price per child per year, and the category Curriculum.
4. Apply through the Odyssey TEFA vendor application; the Comptroller reviews the vendor and each offering. Read Odyssey's vendor articles first, including the fingerprinting and professional license pages, to confirm what applies to a software curriculum.
5. Set up order fulfillment: when an order arrives, send the family its license code the same day.

## Product changes, in order

1. **Free pre-K and kindergarten.** Every pre-K 3, pre-K 4 and kindergarten module stays open to everyone (86 modules), with no code.
2. **License codes for grade 1 and up.** A code signed with iECHO's private key and checked inside the app with the matching public key, so it works offline and cannot be forged; one code per child per school year, matching how the accounts fund each child. The private key never goes in the repository.
3. **A Texas page inside the app.** What is included, the TEKS alignment summary, and the privacy promise, written for parents deciding in the marketplace.
4. **Paint pre-K and kindergarten first.** The free grades are the storefront; their pictures come before any other grade's.
5. Then continue the story doubling from grade 6 up, and after approval, the family web checkout, microschools and app stores.
