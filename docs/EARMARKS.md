## Pre-K 3 and pre-K 4 by the 2027 recommendations (pass MR)

Mikey adopted the 2027 Pre-K Guidelines Review Committee's recommendations in pass MR: "I think we should go ahead and implement the new recommendations from the 2027 Pre-K Guidelines Review Committee.  This app will take years to develop so we might as well adopt the new stuff now."
