# What's new

The newest block is what educators see once, in a pop-up on the classroom page, after each update. Keep each item one plain sentence. The date line is the version stamp.

- Pictures with words: math, science and history lessons show a drawing under the idea, with the formula kept.
- Let's Play: games beside Let's Color, one more for every course mastered.

- Eight little stories for the youngest, and history's third ways come with timelines and maps.

- Every lesson can show its idea another way, a wrong answer offers it too, and the report opens with what changed since you last looked.
- History and reading lessons can show their idea another way too, some in three ways, and Who Needs Help prints every weekly note at once.
- Every math and science lesson can show its idea another way, the weekly note can be edited, and the word pages spell cat, sun and fish.

- Coloring letters are drawn the same on every device, and a story can be printed from its page.
