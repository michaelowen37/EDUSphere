## When they are built

As read from Mikey's words after pass KC: the kindergarten electives finish the full standard first (the review under way), then these are built, so kindergarten is complete, standards and all, before grade 1 begins. Mikey can move the line; if he does, say so here. Each is built to the full standard (docs/FABLE-REVIEW.md), with its codes read in full in the published text, and marked done here with its pass.

Before building one, check whether a kindergarten course already teaches part of it (the electives still under review may, for instance Me and my mind teaches the five senses) and narrow the earmark rather than teach it twice.
