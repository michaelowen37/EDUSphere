## I Social and Emotional Development Domain
## I.A Self-Concept
I.A.1	is aware of where own body is in space and respects personal boundaries.
I.A.2	shows self-awareness of physical attributes, personal preferences, and own abilities.
I.A.3	shows reasonable opinion of his own abilities and limitations.
I.A.4	shows initiative in trying new activities and demonstrates perseverance when attempting to overcome obstacles or challenges.
## I.B Self-Regulation
## I.B.1 Behavior Control
I.B.1.a	follows classroom rules and routines with occasional reminders from adults.
I.B.1.b	takes care of and manages classroom materials.
I.B.1.c	regulates own behavior with occasional reminders or assistance from adults.
## I.B.2 Emotional Control
I.B.2.a	begins to understand the connection between emotions and behaviors.
I.B.2.b	uses verbal and nonverbal communication to communicate basic emotions and feelings.
I.B.2.c	is able to manage intensity of emotions more consistently, although adult guidance is sometimes necessary.
## I.B.3 Control of Attention
I.B.3.a	sustains attention to personally chosen or routine (teacher-directed) tasks until completed.
I.B.3.b	remains focused on engaging, teacher-led group activities for up to 20 minutes.
## I.C Relationships with Others
I.C.1	uses effective verbal and nonverbal communication skills to build relationships with adults and peers.
I.C.2	assumes various roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community.
I.C.3	shows competence in initiating social interactions.
I.C.4	increasingly interacts with peers during cooperative play scenarios that share a common plan and goal.
I.C.5	initiates problem‐solving strategies when experiencing conflicts with others and seeks adult support when necessary.
I.C.6	demonstrates empathy and caring for others.
I.C.7	interacts with peers and has preferred friends.
## I.D Social Awareness
I.D.1	demonstrates an understanding that others have perspectives and feelings that are similar and/or different from her own.
## V Mathematics Domain
## V.A Number Sense
V.A.1	rote counts from 1 to 30.
V.A.2	counts up to 10 objects with one-to-one correspondence.
V.A.3	counts up to 10 items and demonstrates cardinality by communicating that the last number indicates how many items are in the set.
V.A.4	instantly recognizes the quantity of up to 6 objects without counting (subitizes).
V.A.5	recognizes numerals 0-10.
V.A.6	represents quantities up to 10.
V.A.7	begins to understand that numbers 0-10 can be composed and decomposed in various ways to represent a quantity.
V.A.8	compares sets of objects up to 10 using comparative language (e.g., greater/more than, less/fewer than, equal to/same number of).
## V.B Joining and Separating
V.B.1	uses objects, pictorial models, and/or a verbal word problem to represent adding up to 5 objects.
V.B.2	uses objects, pictorial models, and/or a verbal word problem to represent subtracting objects from a set of 5.
## V.C Geometry and Spatial Sense
V.C.1	names and describes common 2D shapes and names at least 1 solid 3D shape.
V.C.2	creates shapes using materials and/or manipulatives.
V.C.3	demonstrates use of position words.
V.C.4	recognizes common shapes, regardless of orientation and size.
## V.D Measurement
V.D.1	recognizes and compares heights or lengths of people or objects.
V.D.2	recognizes and compares capacity based on how much space exists within an object.
V.D.3	recognizes and compares weights of objects.
V.D.4	uses language to describe concepts associated with the passing of time within a day.
## V.E Classification & Patterns
V.E.1	sorts objects that are the same and different into groups and uses language to describe how the groups are similar and different.
V.E.2	collects data and organizes it in a graphic representation.
V.E.3	recognizes, duplicates, extends, and creates patterns.
## VI Science Domain
## VI.A Physical Science
VI.A.1	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses characteristics of common objects.
VI.A.2	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses position and motion of objects.
VI.A.3	uses simple scientific tools to learn about objects.
VI.A.4	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses sources of energy including light, heat, and electricity.
## VI.B Life Science
VI.B.1	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses the characteristics of organisms.
VI.B.2	observes, describes, and discusses the life cycles of organisms.
VI.B.3	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses the relationship of organisms in their environments.
## VI.C Earth & Space Science
VI.C.1	observes, investigates, describes, and discusses earth materials, and their properties and uses.
VI.C.2	identifies, observes, describes, and discusses objects in the sky.
VI.C.3	observes and describes what happens during changes in the earth and sky.
VI.C.4	demonstrates an understanding of the importance of caring for our environment and our planet.
## VII Social Studies Domain
## VII.A People, Past and Present
VII.A.1	identifies similarities and differences between himself, classmates, and other people through specific characteristics and cultural influences.
VII.A.2	identifies similarities and differences in characteristics of families.
VII.A.3	connects his life to events, time, and routines.
## VII.B Economics
VII.B.1	demonstrates an understanding that all people need food, clothing, and shelter.
VII.B.2	demonstrates an understanding of what it means to be a consumer.
VII.B.3	discusses the roles and responsibilities of family, school, and community helpers.
## VII.C Geography
VII.C.1	identifies and creates common geographic features.
VII.C.2	explores geography tools and resources.
## VII.D Citizenship
VII.D.1	identifies the United States and Texas flag.
VII.D.2	recites the Pledge of Allegiance to the United States flag and the Texas flag and observes a moment of silence.
VII.D.3	engages in voting as a method for group decision‐making.
## VIII Fine Arts Domain
## VIII.A Art
VIII.A.1	uses a variety of art materials for sensory experiences and exploration.
VIII.A.2	uses art as a form of creative self‐expression and representation.
VIII.A.3	demonstrates interest in and shows appreciation for the creative work of others.
## VIII.B Music
VIII.B.1	participates in classroom music activities including singing, playing musical instruments, and moving to rhythms.
VIII.B.2	responds to different musical styles through movement and play.
## VIII.C Dramatic Expression
VIII.C.1	creates or recreates stories, moods, or experiences through dramatic representations.
## IX Physical Development Domain
## IX.A Gross Motor Development
IX.A.1	demonstrates coordination and balance in isolation.
IX.A.2	coordinates sequence of movements to perform tasks.
## IX.B Fine Motor Development
IX.B.1	shows control of tasks that require small-muscle strength and control.
IX.B.2	shows increasing control of tasks that require eye-hand coordination.
## IX.C Personal Safety and Health
IX.C.1	practices good habits of personal safety.
IX.C.2	practices good habits of personal health and hygiene.
IX.C.3	identifies good habits of nutrition and exercise.
## X Technology Domain
## X.A Technology and Devices
X.A.1	opens and navigates through digital learning applications and programs, when appropriate.
X.A.2	uses and names a variety of digital tools that support and enhance learning.
X.A.3	uses digital learning applications to contribute to class-made digital products that express own ideas, as appropriate.
X.A.4	uses technology to access appropriate information, with adult assistance.
X.A.5	practices safe behavior while using digital tools and resources.
