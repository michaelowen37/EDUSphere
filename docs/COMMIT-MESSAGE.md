Younger psychology, and speech from kindergarten to grade 12

- Pass GN: grade 6 gets How people work for grades 6 to 8 (psychology-6), built above the Texas minimum: the brain you are growing, attention, memory and practice, feelings as signals, and other people, with the grade 6 health education mental health strands and the CASEL framework alongside, four short stories and a long one about Mike and a team that could not remember the plays, two Wonder questions, and Hold It in Mind, a new kind of game.
- Pass GO: grade 3 gets How your mind works for grades 3 to 5 (psychology-3), built above the Texas minimum: your brain and your senses, how remembering works, big feelings, and friends and other minds, with the grade 3 health education mental health strands and the CASEL framework alongside, four short stories and a long one about Chloe and a worry jar, two Wonder questions, and Name the Feeling, a new kind of game.
- Pass GP: kindergarten gets Me and my mind for kindergarten through grade 2 (psychology-k, read aloud, free): my brain and my senses, remember it, my feelings, and making friends, with the kindergarten health education mental health strands and the CASEL framework alongside, four short stories and a long one about a rainy day club, four coloring pages, two Wonder questions, and Which Sense, a new kind of game.
- Pass GQ: the sixth strand begins with Say it so it lands for grades 9 to 12 (speech-9, the new Speech subject): the communication process, listening that works, building a talk, and delivery and nerves, with the Texas Communication Applications standard and the Common Core speaking and listening standards alongside, four short stories and a long one about Mike at the city council, two Wonder questions, and Cut the Fillers, a new kind of game.
- Pass GR: grade 6 gets Say what you mean for grades 6 to 8 (speech-6), built above the Texas minimum: listening on purpose, saying it clearly, a two-minute talk, and disagreeing out loud, with the grade 6 oral language standards and the Common Core speaking and listening standards alongside, four short stories and a long one about Georgette and a Saturday club, two Wonder questions, and Fit the Room, a new kind of game.
- Pass GS: grade 3 gets Listen, then say it for grades 3 to 5 (speech-3), built above the Texas minimum: listening and asking, telling it in order, a one-minute talk, and taking turns to talk, with the grade 3 oral language standards and the Common Core speaking and listening standards alongside, four short stories and a long one about Savanah and a fire chief, two Wonder questions, and Ask the Right Question, a new kind of game.
- Pass GT: kindergarten gets I can listen, I can say for K to 2 (speech-k, read aloud and free), built above the Texas minimum: my listening body, first, then, do it, my clear voice, and my turn and kind words, with the kindergarten oral language standards and the Common Core speaking and listening standards alongside, four read-aloud stories with coloring pages and a long one about a circle that learned to listen, two Wonder questions, and Whose Turn Next, a new kind of game for small hands; the speech strand is complete. Every new block carries a plain-English explanation, the standing rule from here on.
