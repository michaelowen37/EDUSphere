Philosophy for 9 to 12 and 6 to 8: a new subject, two courses, two games

- Pass GI: the fourth strand of the depth program begins with How to think about anything for grades 9 to 12 (philosophy-9, the new Philosophy subject): what an argument is, the usual mistakes, what we can know, and how we should live, with the Texas College and Career Readiness cross-disciplinary standards and the Common Core argument standards alongside, four short stories and a long one about Frederick and a copper bracelet, two Wonder questions, and Valid or Not, a new kind of game.
- Pass GJ: grade 6 gets Big questions, good reasons for grades 6 to 8 (philosophy-6): questions that open, reasons and evidence, disagreeing well, and fair and unfair, with the Texas College and Career Readiness cross-disciplinary standards and the Common Core grade 6 standards alongside, four short stories and a long one about Savanah and a cabin of ten-year-olds, two Wonder questions, and Reason or Not, a new kind of game.
