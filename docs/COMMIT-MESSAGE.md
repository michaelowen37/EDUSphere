- Pass KI: Money and me (business, K to 2) at the full standard. The banks grow from 20 listed questions to 39 beside the computed sums.
- Pass KJ: Looking and listening (art and music, K to 2) at the full standard. Each lesson has a spoken script; form and balance taught, as the Texas art standard names them; woodwinds and brass inside the wind family; rhythm as long and short sounds; the audience rule matches the listening lesson; two paintings logged; the NCAS music rows quoted exactly; two colons gone from the long story. The banks grow from 24 questions to 45. Every kindergarten course, core and elective, is now at the full standard. The unattended-run files are removed.
